# Product: HeatSink

One question: "If an AI data center on the Massachusetts coast used seawater cooling, where would its waste heat do the least ecological harm?"

## Why now
Massachusetts Executive Order 658 (Sept 8, 2026) requires state guidance by Dec 31, 2026 to help municipalities evaluate data center proposals, and MassDEP protocols on water resources. Seawater cooling avoids freshwater use during drought, but returns heat to an ocean whose habitats are already warm.

## Users
Town boards and planners, state reviewers, fishers and fisheries advocates.

## Honesty rules (apply to every screen and every generated text)
- HeatSink is a SCREENING tool, not a hydrodynamic model or a permit decision. Say so wherever results appear.
- The tool must be able to say "Low impact at screening level". It is not anti-AI.
- Label every layer MEASURED or MODELED. FIShBOT is a blended grid, not a thermometer.
- Missing values are null, never 0. Show gaps; never interpolate silently.
- Never claim a warming trend from a one-week sample.
- Every number on screen names its source. Every species threshold has a citation.
- Placeholder calibration constants are shown as assumptions.
